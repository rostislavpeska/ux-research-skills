=== COLUMN overview | GOMS overview · T1
title: Railo — GOMS test design · T1 “Buy a ticket” (fictional app)
context: Task T1: buy a ticket Prague → Brno for tomorrow around 7:30, window seat · two methods walked on a 1440 × 860 desktop viewport · KLM, expert user, error-free · model: goms-testing/examples/railo/model.yaml, numbers from klm.py. The output is a set of hypotheses for the sessions, never findings.
=== CARD grey | Time per method (KLM)
A · Search form — 9 steps · 23.1 s nominal · high 27.8 s · reported range 18–34 s
B · AI assistant — 6 steps · 22.7 s nominal · high 24.1 s · reported range 18–29 s · 10.4 s without the 44 typed characters
Ratio form / assistant = 1.02: a tie on paper. 2.2 if the user does not have to type (for example dictates).
=== CARD red | Critical points ⚠ — what to watch in the sessions
⚠1 Form, step 5 · The form never asks for the date. The only control is a 24 px calendar icon on the results, ~700 px from the cursor. Expert path 2.65 s; a first-time user has to search. Watch: time from Search to the calendar click.
⚠2 Form, step 9 · The confirmation screen in the prototype shows the time but not the date (payment itself not executed). Watch: does the user check the date afterwards?
⚠3 Assistant, step 2 · 12.3 of the 22.7 s are typing. The method ratio depends on typing speed and on how long the request is. Watch: characters typed and typing time.
=== CARD amber | Selection rules (hypotheses for the session script)
Form: users who know the route and want control; the risk is the date.
Assistant: users who describe a goal in one sentence; needs trust in the answer. Relevance of the answers was not assessed (no message sent).
Ask after both tasks: “Which way would you use tomorrow morning, and why?”
=== CARD grey | Model debt — what this model cannot tell you
Expert and error-free: no searching, no mistakes, no learning. Real users are slower; the ratio is the headline, not the seconds.
System response (R) is left out; measure the assistant's answer time separately (measure_r.py on a live app).
Pay and Send were modelled, NOT EXECUTED (state changes).
M placement follows references/m-rules.md; the scroll in the high estimate is an assumption.
=== NOTE
Status pills: OK = routine step · Borderline = cost or risk worth watching · Problem = critical point ⚠ · Info = modelled but not executed, or not timed. Markers: numbered red circle = click (outline only = modelled, NOT EXECUTED), red dashed box = where attention must go, dashed arrow = mouse path, blue dashed box = system output.
=== STEP ops | Info
title: Where the seconds go — operators per method
M = mental act 1.35 s · P = point 1.1 s · B = button press or release 0.1 s (a click = BB) · H = hands to keyboard or mouse 0.4 s · K = keystroke 0.28 s.
Form: 8 M · 8 P · 16 B · 2 H · 4 K = 23.1 s.
Assistant: 4 M · 3 P · 6 B · 2 H · 45 K = 22.7 s.
The form pays in decisions and pointing; the assistant pays in typing.

# Session script – <STUDY> (<length> min)

The moderator's script for one moderated usability session. The analysis reads it through the study
brief: blocks become board sections, ★ questions are checked for adherence, ⚠ points are the
hypotheses to confirm, and the expected answers decide task success. Write it in the participants'
language.

**★** = always ask · *○* = if time allows · `[ ]` = instruction for the moderator, not read aloud.
After each answer wait 3–5 s in silence. Don't advise, don't judge. Probes: "Tell me more." ·
"When did that last happen?" · "Can you give an example?"

**Never say:** <words that lead or teach: "our app", "correct", product jargon the participant
should discover on their own, …>

## 1 · Intro — 0:00–0:03
`[Recording ready but not started. Empty private browser window.]`
- Who you are; what happens today; "we test the product, not you"; think aloud; you won't help
  during tasks; no personal data, no logging into own accounts.
- Consent to recording. `[Start recording.]` "The recording is running — do you agree?"

## 2 · Interview — 0:03–0:10
- ★ <background question 1>
- ★ <current behaviour: where / how often / what annoys you>
- ★ <last concrete occurrence>
- *○ <optional questions>*
`[0:10 end. Observer notes the participant segment: <A / B / C>.]`

## 3 · Personas — 0:10–0:14
`[Show the persona cards. Don't read them aloud.]`
"Most people are a mix — read them and tell me where you recognise yourself."
- ★ How much of each is in you?
- ★ Where are you different from all of them?
- ★ A situation when you behaved like <the dominant one>?
`[The card wording must match: ask for a mix, not "which one are you".]`

## 4 · Website — 0:14–0:20
`[5-second test, then free exploration.]`
- ★ What do you remember? What is it, and who is it for?
- ★ Before clicking: what do you expect to happen?
- ★ Would you trust it with <data>? Why?
- ★ What would you do now — try it, register, leave?
`[0:19 app not opened → "Let's look inside — which way would you go?" Never say "open the app".]`

## 5 · App, first steps — 0:20–0:28
- ★ What does this screen tell you? · ★ What would you do first?
- After each onboarding step, one question: <step → question>.

## 6 · Tasks — 0:28–0:40
"Now a few things that build on each other. Keep thinking aloud."

**6.1 <Task name> (<budget> min)**
"<Instruction read aloud, as a goal, without UI words>" `[measure time · after <n> min without
progress, show <hint>]`
1. ⚠ <critical point predicted by the GOMS model> → <question at that moment>
   D: <probe — only after the task>
2. <step> → <question>
Expected answer: `[<what counts as success>]`
After the task: <which step was hardest?>

**6.2 …**

`[Checkpoints: 0:32 end of 6.1 · … · 0:40 always go to block 7.]`

## 7 · Closing — 0:40–0:45
- ★ What stuck with you most today? · ★ Would you use it? For what?
- **Rating, silent technique (~2 min):** `[Give the rating card. Stay silent.]` <n> statements, 1–10,
  "—" if not done today. Only for the lowest number: "Why exactly that much?"
- Reveal anything withheld for neutrality; observer's questions; thanks; reward.
`[Stop recording. Fresh guest session for the next participant.]`

### Example task (fictional)

**6.1 Import your data (4 min)** — "You have sample data here. Replace it with your own three
files so that only yours remain." `[after 2 min without progress, point to the settings icon]`
1. ⚠ Where to start (settings icon has no label) → "Where would you look?"
2. ⚠ After confirming, nothing visible changes → "Is it there? How can you tell?"
Expected answer: `[only the three uploaded accounts are listed; sample accounts are gone]`
